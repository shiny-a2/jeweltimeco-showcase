# Official campaigns in the JewelTime storefront

JewelTime's brand index now uses the watch brands' own logos and verified campaign imagery. Tornado's red-car watch campaign appears on the homepage and its brand page. Visitors can select a brand and browse its currently listed women's and men's models without leaving the homepage; the controls move manually and remain usable on narrow screens.

The live presentation was checked across the homepage, a brand archive, shop and contact pages at mobile and desktop widths in both browser color preferences. Campaign images loaded, model rows used current catalogue entries, the mobile menu and carousel controls worked, and no horizontal overflow was observed. No purchase or inquiry was submitted. Some brands do not yet have a verified suitable campaign image, and one catalogue category currently has no models to show.
