# JewelTime storefront refinement

The storefront homepage now leads with JewelTime's logo and the slogan «زمان جواهر است». A separate Tornado campaign retains the brand's red-car watch imagery. Model carousels display current public catalogue prices, with an inquiry label where no price is listed. Shop and brand pages provide clearer paths into available brands and women's and men's models. The single product page and the existing account login form received a cleaner presentation; the former blank login route now opens that form.

Final polish shows all ten shop brand logos in a complete desktop grid, keeps the desktop menu on one line, and contains the product specifications within the page at intermediate widths.

Live browser checks covered the homepage, shop, Tornado archive, one product and account entry at mobile and desktop widths in both light and dark preferences. The product page was also checked at an intermediate width. The inspected pages loaded, the model prices and product gallery were visible, and tested carousel, navigation, gallery and password controls responded. The final product checks measured no horizontal overflow. No purchase, message or login was submitted. Authenticated account pages remain untested.
